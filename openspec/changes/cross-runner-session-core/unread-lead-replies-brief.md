## Outcome
An informational Lead reply must remain discoverable until actually displayed to the user, even when subsequent independent work completes. This complements decisions; reading is not answering or approving.

## Acceptance
- Durable individually identified completed user-facing Lead replies, including direct investigations without child integration and replies while other tasks run. Do not manufacture unread entries for internal notices, tool output or transient progress. Preserve known native-phase limitations honestly.
- Compact unread count opens a list with bounded specific summaries and navigation to the exact reply in paginated chat. Preserve drafts and session identity. Empty state consumes no extra panel space.
- Later replies, topic changes, user prompts, synthetic wakes, page reload and daemon restart do not mark earlier replies read or discard their attention.
- Receipt is tied to displaying the specific reply in a visible, focused viewport, never session open, push delivery/click or latest event cursor alone. No claim of human comprehension. Long replies must not require impossible whole-message visibility.
- Push links to exact reply, including cold start, existing window, paginated history and legacy worker fallback. Preserve current independent delivery, dedupe, opt-out, authorization, retry and storage protections. Never resolve decisions/approvals by reading.
- Choose explicit reader/device scope consistent with existing per-device viewing; do not conflate device visibility with every user's read state. Document it. Existing history migration must avoid flooding unread/push counts.
- Bounded query/pagination/index costs, durable accurate identity, no lossy latest-turn replacement. No new settings or model calls.
